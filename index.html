What is your discipline policy? What happens if my child bites or hits?

We practice 'positive guidance,' not 'discipline' or 'punishment.' We never use time-outs. Our teachers are trained to get on the child's level, help them name their big feelings (e.g., "I see you are frustrated"), and redirect them to a positive solution. For behaviors like biting or hitting, our first priority is the safety and comfort of all children.
